How do I sign up for my first course?
  1. On Smartbrain Academy, enter the subject you want to learn and click Search.
  2. Find a tutor who meets your needs from the search results page. You can message tutors directly from their profiles.
  3. Use the filtering options on the left side of your search results page. When you’re exploring your options for the perfect tutor, you can customize your tutor search with a variety of options, including Subject, Hourly Rate, tutors opted into Instant Book, Online/In-person, Distance, Tutor Credentials, Additional languages spoken, Student’s level, and Tutor age.
